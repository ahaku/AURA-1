A small instrument. An endless composition.

Music that
grows itself.

AURA-1 turns your FM-1 into a living ambient instrument. Plant a few notes, play along, and let it find its own way.

AURA-1 is a modified copy of Charles Vestal's AMB-1. It adds Solo Mode for live polyphonic playing and Random World, a preset randomizer. Original AMB-1 on GitHub.
